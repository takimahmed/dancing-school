Anfahrt

Vier Wege
zur Fläche.

Bornheim is the most reachable neighborhood in Frankfurt. Pick the option that fits your evening — most students take the U-Bahn.

01

By U-Bahn

U4 to Bornheim Mitte (2 min walk) or U4/U7 to Seckbacher Landstraße (5 min). The studio is on the south side of Berger Straße — exit toward Saalburgallee.

02

By Bike

Covered bike racks at the studio entrance. Direct cycle path from Konstablerwache via Berger Straße — about 12 minutes from the center.

03

By Car

Limited street parking on Saalburgstraße and Habsburgerallee (free after 19:00). Public garage at Bornheim Mitte — €1.50/hr.

04

On Foot

20 minutes from Konstablerwache through the old Bornheim village. The walk down Berger Straße is half the joy of coming.

05

Accessibility

Step-free entrance from the street. Studio floor and changing rooms on ground level. Accessible toilet. Message us before your trial if we can help with anything specific.

06

Where exactly

The entrance is at the corner of Alt-Bornheim and Saalburgstraße. Look for the dark green door with the brass plate. Buzzer marked "Easy Dance" — second from top.

Studio innen

aus der Nähe.

220 m² of original wooden floor, a mirror wall, two changing rooms, a lounge corner, and the warmest light in Bornheim after 19:00.

Wo essen
before & after.

amp; danach.

Ein paar Adressen auf der Berger Straße, die man kennen sollte. Die meisten fünf Minuten zu Fuß vom Studio. Schnelle Snacks vor der Stunde, Wein nach dem Freitags-Social.

Coffee

Sugar Mama

Best coffee on Berger Straße. Open until 18:00 on weekdays — great for a flat white before evening class. Two minutes from the studio.

Lunch

Bornheim Taqueria

Mexican tacos, fast service. Three minutes' walk. Eduardo's pick on a Tuesday evening before teaching.

Dinner

Trattoria Adriatico

Family-run Italian, on the corner of Berger Straße and Höhenstraße. Open late. Where the Friday Social often migrates to at 1 a.m.

Wine bar

Apfelweinwirtschaft Wagner

The classic Frankfurt Apfelwein experience, two minutes north. Loud, warm, traditional. Good first-time-in-Frankfurt stop.

Bakery

Wöbelt Bäckerei

Old-school German bakery. Open at 06:00 if you're an absurdly early Saturday class person. Five minutes' walk.

Late night

Bar Plank

Cocktail bar, opens at 18:00. Where post-Friday-Social conversations stretch into the morning. Three minutes from the studio.
