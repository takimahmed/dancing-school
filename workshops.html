Was ist ein Wochenend-Workshop?

Ein fokussierter
Deep-Dive.

A weekend workshop is a Saturday-Sunday block of six hours total — three hours per day — on one single theme.

Where weekly classes build progressively, workshops go deep on one thing: Bachata Sensual body movement, Zouk musicality, Salsa shines, Tarraxinha technique. You leave with a deep, specific skill instead of a broad survey.

Most weekends sell out two weeks ahead. Members save 10% and can defer once if life intervenes. Drop-ins welcome too — workshops are a great way to meet the studio without committing to a course.

The five questions we get most often. WhatsApp us for anything else — usually a one-hour reply.

Was ist der Unterschied zwischen Workshop und Kurs?
A workshop is a single weekend on one focused theme. A course is an 8–10 week block that builds a broader skill set. Workshops are great for visitors, dancers with experience already, or anyone wanting to go deep on one specific technique fast.
Brauche ich eine:n Partner:in?
No. Workshops use rotation just like classes. Solo dancers welcome and outnumber couples most weekends.
Bekomme ich eine Erstattung, falls ich nicht kommen kann?
Full refund up to seven days before. After that, we'll credit you toward a future workshop. Members can defer once at no charge.
Gibt es einen Mitglieder-Rabatt?
Yes — Studio Members get 10% off every workshop. Discount applied automatically when you book through your member login.
Wie ist es mit Essen und Pausen?
Every workshop weekend has a 75-minute lunch break and two 15-minute breaks. We provide tea, coffee, water, and fruit. Schertlinstraße has a dozen lunch spots within five minutes.
